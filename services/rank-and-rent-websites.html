Rank and rent websites built for one niche in one city

A rank and rent site is a small property that earns local enquiries and is leased to a business that wants them. We build the site, set the SEO foundations and make the whole thing easy to hand over.

What makes a good rental site

The best ones are narrow. One trade, one city, a handful of pages and a clear call path. That focus makes the site easier to rank and easier to describe to a renter.

We keep the technology light, so hosting is cheap and the site stays fast.

What we build for you

  • A compact site structure: home, services, area page and contact.
  • Original copy for the niche and city, with no borrowed text.
  • On-page SEO, local schema and a sitemap ready for Search Console.
  • Call tracking and a form that routes enquiries to the renter.
  • A simple handover pack: logins, notes and a list of what to update.

Plan the handover early

A renter wants to know three things: what traffic the site gets, where the calls come from and what it takes to keep it going. We build reporting in from the start so those answers are easy.

We also write a short maintenance note that covers hosting, domain renewal and the content that may need updating each season.
