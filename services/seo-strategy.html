SEO strategy services turn a pile of possible tasks into one ranked plan. For local service businesses, that plan says which pages to build, which searches to chase and what to fix first, so effort goes toward calls and form leads instead of vanity metrics.

What an SEO strategy includes

  • A review of your current rankings, traffic and the pages that already earn calls, so the plan builds on what works.
  • Keyword and service-area targets mapped to specific pages, including the ones you do not have yet.
  • A ranked fix list covering technical problems, thin pages, missing schema markup and Google Business Profile gaps.
  • A competitor comparison for your two or three real local rivals, not a generic top-ten list.
  • A plan for AI answers as well as blue links, so ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can describe you correctly.
  • A simple scorecard of rankings, calls and form leads to check each month.

How it works

  1. 01

    Read the whole picture

    We look at the site, Google Search Console data, your Google Business Profile and the way you appear in search and AI answers today.

  2. 02

    Find the leaks

    Calls are lost in specific places: a service with no page, a slow mobile site, a profile with the wrong category. We list each one.

  3. 03

    Rank by impact

    Every item is scored by how much it should affect leads against how long it takes, then ordered into a plan.

  4. 04

    Hand it over or build it

    Take the plan to your own team, or have Callworthy AEO carry it out and report back monthly.

Questions about SEO Strategy

What is the difference between SEO strategy and an SEO audit?

An audit finds problems. A strategy decides what to do about them and in what order, and adds the pages, keywords and content you still need. Many clients start with an audit and move into a strategy once the problems are clear.

Do I need a local SEO consultant or a freelance SEO consultant?

The title matters less than who does the work. Callworthy AEO is a small practice where the person who writes the strategy also carries out the fixes, which avoids the usual hand-off between an account manager and a junior team.

Can you promise a ranking?

No. Nobody controls Google. The plan is built on the factors you can control, and each month we measure what moved so the next step is based on evidence.
