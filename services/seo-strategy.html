Why a plan comes before the work

Most service businesses have tried SEO in pieces: a few blog posts, a profile edit, a plugin install. Each piece was reasonable alone. Together they never added up, because nobody decided what mattered most.

Our strategy work puts that decision on paper. It names the services that pay the bills, the towns worth chasing and the technical blockers that would waste any content added on top.

What the written strategy contains

  • A snapshot of where you rank today, which pages earn visits and which of those visits turn into calls.
  • A page map: every service and area you want to win, matched to an existing page or flagged as a gap.
  • A short list of technical and profile fixes that must happen before new content will help.
  • A comparison against the two or three rivals that actually take your jobs.
  • Goals for AI answers, including which questions your pages should be able to settle.
  • A simple monthly scorecard: positions, calls and form leads.

How it runs

  1. 01

    Gather

    Site crawl, Search Console, your business profile and live checks of the searches your customers type.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    We separate what is broken from what is merely missing, because the fixes differ.

  3. 03

    Sequence

    Each task gets a rough effort and a rough payoff, and the plan is ordered from there.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Your team can run the plan, or we can carry it out and report monthly.

Who it fits

Owners and marketing leads who have spent money on SEO tasks without a clear order of attack, and businesses leaving an agency that reported activity rather than calls.

Questions about SEO Strategy

How is a strategy different from an audit?

An audit lists what is wrong today. A strategy goes further and decides what to build, what to ignore and what sequence gives the best return on your time.

Will the plan promise rankings?

No. Rankings depend on Google and on competitors. The plan commits to actions we control and to measurements you can check.

How long is the plan useful?

Most plans guide about two quarters of work. We revisit it when your services, areas or competitors change.
