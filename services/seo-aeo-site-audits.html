What a website SEO audit covers

  • Technical SEO audit services: crawling, indexing, canonicals, redirects, sitemaps, robots.txt and Core Web Vitals.
  • On-page review of your main service and area pages for answers, headings, internal links and thin content.
  • A local SEO audit covering Google Business Profile categories, services, photos, listings and name, address and phone consistency.
  • An AI visibility audit: how ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews currently describe you.
  • Schema markup validation and a note on which types are missing.
  • A one-page summary, then the detailed fix list ordered by impact and effort.

How it works

  1. 01

    Crawl and collect

    We crawl the site, pull Search Console data and review the business profile and listings.

  2. 02

    Test the money paths

    We follow the route a visitor takes from search result to call or form, on mobile first, and note where it breaks.

  3. 03

    Rank the findings

    Each finding is scored on likely lead impact and effort, and anything that does not matter is left out.

  4. 04

    Walk through it

    You receive the fix list in writing. Take it to your developer or have Callworthy AEO make the changes.

Who it is for

Audits suit businesses whose traffic has dropped, sites that have been rebuilt or migrated, owners about to hire an agency who want an independent view, and agencies that need an audit for a prospect or client.

Questions about SEO and AEO Site Audits

How is this different from an automated SEO report?

Automated tools list every warning. A person reads the results, throws out the noise and ranks what is left by effect on leads, so you get a list you can act on.

Do you audit for AI visibility as well?

Yes. The AI visibility audit records whether AI tools mention you, what they say and which pages they cite, alongside the standard checks.

Will the audit fix anything?

The audit finds and ranks the problems. If you want the fixes made, say so and the work follows the same list.
