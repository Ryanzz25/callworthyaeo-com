Convex integrations that keep tools and dashboardsup to date live

Convex integrations add real-time data to a website, so tools and dashboards update the moment something changes. Callworthy AEO uses Convex when a service business or lead project needs live lead boards, shared status views or interactive tools that cannot rely on a page reload.

What a Convex integration includes

  • Data models for the records your tool or dashboard needs, such as leads, jobs, call outcomes or quote requests.
  • Live queries, so a new lead or a status change appears on every open screen without refreshing.
  • Server functions that validate input and apply business rules before data is saved.
  • A dashboard or board view built for the people who use it, with filters for what they check most.
  • Connections to forms, call tracking and your CRM where needed.
  • Documentation and a handover that keeps the project in your account.

How it works

  1. 01

    Decide if live data is needed

    Many sites do not need it. We check that real-time updates solve a real problem before building.

  2. 02

    Model the data

    Tables and rules are planned around the actions people take, such as claim, call back and close.

  3. 03

    Build the live views

    Screens subscribe to the data and update as it changes, tested with several users at once.

  4. 04

    Connect and hand over

    Inputs and outputs are wired to your other systems, and you receive access and a written guide.

Who it is for

This fits teams that coordinate leads or jobs among several people, projects with interactive tools that show shared results, and lead generation operators who want a live view of incoming calls and forms. For a simple contact form, it is more than you need.

Questions about Convex Integrations

When should I choose Convex over Supabase?

Convex suits live, shared views where updates must appear at once. Supabase suits a conventional database with admin views and exports. We help you pick, and sometimes the answer is neither.

Does it affect SEO?

The public marketing pages stay static and fast. Live data is used inside tools and dashboards, which usually do not need to be indexed.

Can it connect to my existing site?

Yes. The live part is added to the pages that need it, without rebuilding the rest of the site.
