AI search optimization services that get younamed in AI answers

AI search optimization services help your business show up when people ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews for a recommendation. This is generative engine optimization (GEO): making your brand a clear, trusted source those systems can name, for local service businesses and the agencies that serve them.

What AI SEO services cover

  • A visibility benchmark across the main AI tools for the questions your buyers ask, repeated so you can see movement.
  • Page changes that support ChatGPT SEO and LLM SEO: direct answers, clear service definitions and area coverage.
  • AI Overviews optimization, including which of your pages are cited and which are skipped.
  • Trusted mention building: the directories, profiles and sites that models and search tools repeat.
  • Technical access checks so AI crawlers can read your pages, with robots.txt and markup reviewed.
  • A plain-language report on how to rank in ChatGPT for your trade, based on what we see for your market.

How it works

  1. 01

    Benchmark

    We run a fixed set of buyer questions through each AI tool and log mentions, competitors named and sources cited.

  2. 02

    Find the gaps

    Missing pages, vague wording, blocked crawlers and unclear business details are the usual reasons a brand is skipped.

  3. 03

    Fix and earn mentions

    Pages are rewritten, markup is added and accurate listings are built on sites that the tools draw from.

  4. 04

    Repeat and compare

    The benchmark is re-run monthly. Answers vary between runs, so we report patterns rather than single screenshots.

Who it is for

This service is for local service businesses and agencies that want to be recommended, not only ranked. If you have searched your own trade in ChatGPT and found competitors named instead of you, or nothing at all, this is the place to start. Agencies can buy it as a white-label add-on.

Questions about AI Search Optimization (GEO)

How do I rank in ChatGPT?

There is no ranking list to join. ChatGPT answers draw on pages it can read and sources it trusts. You improve your odds with clear pages, consistent business details and accurate mentions elsewhere, then check the answers regularly.

Is GEO different from AEO?

The overlap is large. AEO leans toward featured answers and voice results, while GEO focuses on how language models describe and recommend brands. We run them as one workstream because the same fixes support both.

Do you work as a GEO agency for other agencies?

Yes. Agencies can use the benchmarking and implementation under their own brand. See white label AEO services for how that works.
