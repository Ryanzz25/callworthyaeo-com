Keyword research services show what your customers type, how often and how hard it is to rank, before you spend on pages. Callworthy AEO researches service, area and question searches for local businesses and maps each one to a page, so every page has a reason to exist.

What the keyword research includes

  • Search volume and competition data for your services, cities and neighborhoods, pulled for the US or Australia as needed.
  • Intent labels on each term: ready to hire, comparing options, or just learning.
  • Question keywords for FAQs and AI answers, including how-to and cost-style questions where they apply.
  • A map from every keyword group to an existing page or a page to build.
  • A competitor check to see who holds the top local positions and what their pages offer.
  • A short priority list that puts the highest-value, winnable terms first.

How it works

  1. 01

    Collect the seed list

    We start from your services, service areas and the words customers use when they call.

  2. 02

    Pull the data

    Volume, competition and related searches are gathered and grouped into topics.

  3. 03

    Check the results page

    We look at who ranks, whether Maps results dominate and whether AI answers appear.

  4. 04

    Map and prioritize

    Each group is matched to a page and ranked by value and difficulty, giving you a build order.

Who it is for

This is for owners planning a new site or new service pages, teams about to commission content, and anyone who has been told to target a keyword without seeing the numbers. It is also useful before choosing which cities to serve with location pages.

Questions about Keyword Research

Do keyword volumes matter for local businesses?

They help, but a term with modest volume can still be worth more than a big one if the searcher is ready to book. We weigh intent and job value, not volume alone.

Do you research for AI search as well as Google?

Yes. Question-style searches and the wording buyers use in AI tools are included, because those shape the answers your pages should give.

What do I get at the end?

A spreadsheet of keyword groups with data, intent and the page each belongs on, plus a short written summary of what to do first.
