Local SEO services for companies that serve a territory

Local SEO is about being the obvious pick for a customer a few miles away. As a local SEO company we combine service pages, area coverage, consistent listings and a strong map presence, and we treat each piece as part of one system.

The parts that move local results

Rankings in the map pack depend on relevance, distance and prominence. You cannot change distance, but you can improve the other two with accurate categories, helpful pages and trustworthy mentions.

Consistency matters more than volume. A short list of correct, matching listings beats a hundred sloppy ones.

Where a local SEO expert spends time

  • Area pages that describe real work in real places, not swapped town names.
  • Local citation building services limited to relevant, reputable directories.
  • NAP consistency checks: name, address and phone matched across the web.
  • Local business schema that repeats your details in machine-readable form.
  • Internal links that guide visitors from area pages to services.
  • Tracking of map pack positions across the towns you serve.

Proof beats volume

A local search result is a small trust contest. Photos of your own work, answers to real questions and accurate details all help a customer decide, and a thin page full of town names does not.

We write area content from facts supplied by your team: the streets you work on, the building types you see and the jobs that come up in each place.

Questions about Local SEO

What affects local SEO pricing?

Mainly the number of locations, the number of towns you want to win and how much cleanup is needed. We scope after reviewing your current setup.

Do I need a page for every town?

Only for towns where you do real work and can say something specific. Thin pages hurt more than they help.

How do you handle a service area business with no storefront?

We hide the address if needed, define the service areas carefully and build pages around the territory.
