SEO content strategy that plans pages aroundwhat buyers ask

An SEO content strategy decides which pages your site needs, what each one should answer and how they link together. For service businesses, it replaces random blog posts with a map of service, area and question pages designed to earn rankings, AI citations and calls.

What our content strategy services deliver

  • A content inventory of what exists, what earns traffic and what should be merged, rewritten or removed.
  • A page map: one page per service and service area worth targeting, each with a clear purpose.
  • Question research from search data, People Also Ask, call notes and customer emails.
  • Briefs for each page with the opening answer, required sections, internal links and schema to add.
  • A publishing order based on lead value, so the first pages written are the ones most likely to bring calls.
  • Refresh rules for pages that age, such as pricing context, seasonal advice and service area changes.

How it works

  1. 01

    Inventory

    We list every page, check traffic and rankings, and mark what to keep, fix or retire.

  2. 02

    Map the demand

    Search data and customer questions are grouped into topics, then matched to a page that exists or must be made.

  3. 03

    Write the briefs

    Each brief gives the writer the answer, the sections, the links and the facts to include, so quality does not depend on guesswork.

  4. 04

    Schedule and review

    Pages go out in priority order, and each is reviewed against rankings and leads after it has had time to settle.

Who it is for

This is for businesses with a website but no plan for it: owners who publish occasionally, teams whose blog has outgrown its purpose, and agencies who need a content plan for a client. It works alongside keyword research and is the base for AEO and GEO work.

Questions about Content Strategy

Is content strategy the same as a blog calendar?

No. A calendar lists dates. A strategy decides what the site needs to rank and convert, and many of the best pages are service and area pages, not blog posts.

Do you write the content too?

Yes, if you want it. Pages are written to the brief, reviewed by a person and edited for accuracy. You can also give the briefs to your own writer.

How is content strategy different from keyword research?

Keyword research tells you what people search. Content strategy decides what to publish in response and in what order. The two are usually done together.
