SEO content strategy for businesses that sell by phone

Content for a plumber or a roofer does not look like content for a software company. Buyers want proof, process and a number to call. We plan a body of pages that covers what they ask, in the order that earns attention fastest.

Content with a job to do

Every page we plan has a purpose: win a search, settle an objection or give an assistant a clean answer to quote. Pages without a purpose are dropped from the plan before anyone writes a word.

That discipline keeps the site small enough to maintain and strong enough to rank.

What the plan delivers

  • A topic map grouped by service, area and buyer question.
  • Briefs for each priority page, with the target search, the angle and the facts to include.
  • A publishing order that starts with pages closest to a sale.
  • Guidance on updating or merging old posts that compete with each other.
  • Notes on which pages should carry FAQs, tables or step lists.
  • A review rhythm so the plan adjusts to what actually performs.

Questions come from the front line

The best source of topics is rarely a keyword tool. It is the person who answers the phone. Ask them what callers want to know before booking, and which worries come up again and again.

We fold those answers into the plan, then check them against search demand. The result is a set of pages that reads like your business talking, not like generic filler.

How it runs

  1. 01

    Listen

    We gather the questions your team hears on calls and quote requests.

  2. 02

    Map

    Questions are matched to searches and to pages you have or need.

  3. 03

    Brief

    Each page gets a short brief a writer can follow.

  4. 04

    Review

    We check published pages against the brief and adjust the next batch.

Who it fits

Businesses with a neglected blog, owners preparing to hire a writer, and teams that need a clear brief for every page.

Questions about Content Strategy

Do you write the content too?

We can. Many clients take the briefs to their own writer, and others have us write and publish.

How many pages do I need?

Fewer than you think. The map shows the minimum set that covers your services and areas properly.

Should old blog posts be deleted?

Only when they have no traffic, no links and no purpose. Others are merged or refreshed.
