Google Business Profile optimization is the work of setting up and maintaining your Google listing so it appears in the local pack and Google Maps for the searches that bring calls. Callworthy AEO handles the profile for service businesses: categories, services, service areas, photos, posts and the questions customers ask.

What our GBP optimization services include

  • A full profile audit: primary and secondary categories, services list, service areas, hours, attributes and description.
  • Name, address and phone checks so the profile matches your website exactly.
  • Service and product entries written in the words customers search, not internal jargon.
  • A photo plan covering the team, vehicles, jobs and premises, with filenames and captions to match.
  • Post and Q&A content, plus a simple process for asking real customers for reviews and replying to them.
  • Monthly checks of Maps and local pack positions for your main services and areas.

How it works

  1. 01

    Audit the listing

    We compare your profile with the top local results for your main search and note what they have that you lack.

  2. 02

    Correct the basics

    Categories, services, areas, hours and contact details are fixed first, because errors there hold everything else back.

  3. 03

    Add the signals

    Photos, posts, answers and a review request routine build activity that Google and customers can see.

  4. 04

    Track and adjust

    Position checks run monthly for the searches that matter, and the profile is updated as the business changes.

Questions about Google Business Profile Optimization

What is the difference between a GBP optimization service and local SEO?

The profile is one part of local SEO. Local SEO also covers your website, location pages and listings. Many businesses start with the profile because it is quick to improve and sits on the Maps result itself.

Does Google Maps SEO depend on reviews?

Reviews are one factor among several, alongside categories, proximity and website relevance. We help you ask for genuine reviews and reply to them, and we never write or buy fake ones.

Will AI answers use my profile?

Often, yes. Google AI Overviews and other tools draw on profile data, so a complete, consistent profile supports AEO as well as Maps.
