Google Business Profile optimization for a listing that gets called

For many trades the profile is the first thing a customer sees, ahead of the website. Google business profile management means keeping that listing complete, accurate and active. We set it up properly, then maintain it.

Request a site reviewAll services

Your map listing is a landing page

A listing with the wrong primary category, a thin services list or a stale set of photos still gets views. It just converts fewer of them. GMB optimization is mostly a matter of removing those small, expensive gaps.

The profile also feeds AI answers. Assistants that pull from Maps data repeat whatever your listing says, so errors travel.

What Google business profile management services involve

  • Primary and secondary category review against what competitors use.
  • Service lists, descriptions and attributes written in plain, accurate language.
  • Photo guidance: what to upload, how to name it and what to avoid.
  • Posts, offers and Q&A seeded with real questions customers ask.
  • Hours, holiday hours, service areas and messaging settings checked.
  • A review process that asks real customers and never invents feedback.

Small details that add up

Profile quality is often decided by details that nobody checks: a service area that was never set, an old phone number, a category copied from a competitor. Fixing them takes little time and removes real friction.

We also review the profile each month for suggested edits by the public, which can change your listing without notice.

How it runs

  1. 01

    Inspect

    We compare your listing with the top three results for your main search.

  2. 02

    Correct

    Errors and gaps are fixed, with your approval on anything public.

  3. 03

    Align

    We make the website say the same things as the profile.

  4. 04

    Maintain

    Monthly updates and checks keep the listing from drifting.

Who it fits

Owners with a listing that rarely gets calls, anyone comparing profile managers, businesses with several locations, and anyone who has just inherited a profile created by a previous marketer.
