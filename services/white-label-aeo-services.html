White label SEO services for agencies that needAEO delivery

White label SEO services let a marketing agency sell search work without hiring a specialist. Callworthy AEO delivers local SEO and white label AEO under your brand: audits, AI visibility benchmarking and hands-on implementation, with reports you can hand to your own clients.

What agencies get with white label AEO

  • SEO and AEO site audits formatted so you can present them as your own, with a short ranked fix list.
  • White label local SEO: Google Business Profile work, location pages, citations and review request flows.
  • AI visibility benchmarking across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews for each of your clients.
  • Implementation of page, markup, speed and form changes inside your client sites, or handed to your developer as tickets.
  • Monthly reports without our branding, written so your account manager can explain them.
  • A single point of contact who has done the audit and the fixes, so questions do not bounce between people.

How it works

  1. 01

    Scope a client

    You send the client site and goals. We confirm what is in scope and what you will deliver to the client.

  2. 02

    Audit under your brand

    The review is written as a document you can put your logo on, ranked by lead impact.

  3. 03

    Implement

    Changes are made in the order agreed. Anything needing client approval comes back to you first.

  4. 04

    Report

    Each month you receive rankings, AI answer checks and a note on the next step, ready to forward.

Who it is for

This suits web design shops, PPC agencies, marketing consultancies and IT providers whose clients ask about SEO or AI search and who would rather not build that team. Work is for service businesses in the US and Australia, the same markets we cover directly.
