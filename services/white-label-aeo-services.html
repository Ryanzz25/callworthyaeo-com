White label SEO services and AEO for agencies

Agencies are being asked about AI search by clients who still expect normal SEO results. We act as a quiet delivery partner: you keep the relationship and the branding, and we do the audits, page work and benchmarking behind the scenes.

Request a site reviewAll services

How the partnership runs

You are the face to the client. We never contact your customers unless you ask, and nothing we produce carries our name. Reports, audits and page copy arrive formatted for your use.

We start small, usually with one client site, so you can judge the quality before routing more work to us.

What we can take off your plate

  • Pre-sales audits you can show a prospect, covering rankings, profile health and AI answers.
  • AEO page rewrites and schema, delivered as ready-to-publish text or applied directly.
  • Monthly AI answer benchmarking for a client keyword set.
  • Local SEO tasks such as profile updates and citation clean-up.
  • Plain-language summaries your account managers can send on.

What good delivery looks like

Agencies tell us the same thing: the hard part of white label work is trust. You need files that arrive on time, written in a tone you can send on, with findings you can defend in a client meeting.

So each deliverable ships with the evidence behind it, such as screenshots of assistant answers and lists of affected URLs. If a client asks how we know something, you have the answer ready.

How it runs

  1. 01

Who it fits

Web design shops, PPC agencies and marketing firms that want to add AI search without hiring, and consultants who need overflow capacity.
