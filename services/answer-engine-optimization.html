What AEO actually changes

AEO marketing and AEO optimization are not a separate universe from SEO. They reuse the same crawlable pages, then adds a layer of clarity: one fact per sentence, a direct answer near the top, and markup that confirms who you are.

The part owners overlook is consistency. An assistant that finds three different phone numbers or two different service lists for one company will often skip it. Entity SEO, meaning the tidy, repeated description of your business across the web, fixes that.

Inside an AEO engagement

  • An AEO audit that tests the questions buyers ask against your pages and records who gets named today.
  • Answer-first rewrites of service pages, with a plain definition and a clear next step in the opening lines.
  • FAQ schema and service markup that matches what is visible on each page.
  • Entity clean-up: name, address, phone, categories and service areas aligned everywhere they appear.
  • A question bank for your trade, turned into short pages that settle one question each.

Questions about AEO for Local Businesses

What is AEO?

AEO stands for answer engine optimization. It means structuring your site and listings so search answers and AI assistants can state what you do, where, and how to contact you.

AEO vs SEO vs GEO: which do I need?

They overlap. In SEO vs AEO, SEO earns rankings while AEO earns quotable answers. In AEO vs GEO, GEO focuses on how language models mention brands in longer replies. We plan all three as one set of page and entity improvements.

Does FAQ schema help by itself?

Not alone. It confirms content already on the page. The questions still need good, short answers that a visitor would find useful.

How do you measure an AEO result?

We keep a fixed list of questions, re-ask them periodically and note whether your business is named, linked or described correctly.
