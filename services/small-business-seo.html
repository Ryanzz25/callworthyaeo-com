Small business SEO services sized for owner-led companies

Small business SEO is a different job from enterprise SEO, and SEO services for small business owners should be scoped that way. You do not need forty landing pages and a monthly slide deck. You need the right ten pages, a profile that works and a clear idea of what is paying off.

Doing less, on purpose

When a team is one owner and a part-time office manager, every task has to earn its place. We trim the plan to the actions with the strongest link to enquiries and park the rest.

As an SEO consultant for small business clients, we also explain what we are doing and why, so you stay in control of your own marketing.

A typical small business scope

  • A core set of service pages written around how customers search.
  • A tuned Google Business Profile and matching website details.
  • Basic technical health: speed, mobile layout, indexing.
  • One or two answer-first FAQ pages for assistants to quote.
  • Simple call and form tracking you can read in a few minutes.

What we leave out

Many packages include tasks that look busy but do little for a small company: dozens of directory submissions, weekly blog posts nobody reads, reports full of vanity numbers.

We cut those from the plan, so the budget goes to pages, profile work and fixes that connect to enquiries. If something is not worth your money, we will say so.

How it runs

  1. 01

    Talk

    We learn what you sell, who buys and what is already working.

  2. 02

    Trim

    Anything outside your budget or capacity is left out of scope.

  3. 03

    Do

    We carry out the agreed tasks and keep notes you can read.

  4. 04

    Review

    We meet or write monthly and decide whether to add more.

Who it fits

Sole traders, family companies and small teams who handle their own marketing and want a clear, modest plan rather than a large retainer.

Questions about SEO for Small Business

How much SEO does a small business need?

Enough to cover the services and areas that pay. For many, that is a short list of well-built pages plus a solid listing.

Can I do some of it myself?

Yes. We often hand over simple tasks, such as photo uploads, with instructions.

Do you require long contracts?

We keep arrangements simple and talk through terms when we scope the work.
