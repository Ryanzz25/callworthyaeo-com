Pay per call marketing built on pages thatdrive tracked calls

Pay per call marketing means earning revenue or leads from the phone calls a page produces. Callworthy AEO builds call-focused landing pages with tracking for service businesses and publishers who want pay per call leads they can count, from emergency trades to appointment-based services.

Request a site reviewAll services

What a pay per call setup includes

  • Landing pages built around one job: a visible phone number, a clear offer of what the call is about and short supporting copy.
  • Call tracking numbers per page or source, so each call is tied to where it came from.
  • Click-to-call buttons and sticky mobile bars tested on real phones.
  • Honest page copy: no invented reviews, ratings or claims about licensing or response times that the business cannot back up.
  • Analytics events for call taps and form submissions, sent to your reporting.
  • Search visibility work for the page, covering title, headings, schema markup and local relevance.

Questions about Pay Per Call

Is pay per call the same as rank and rent?

They are related. Rank and rent builds a site that ranks and is then leased to a business. Pay per call focuses on the calls themselves and how they are paid for. Many projects use both ideas.

What is a pay per call lead?

It is a phone call from a prospective customer, qualified by rules such as a minimum duration, which a buyer agrees to pay for. The rules are set between you and the buyer, not by us.

Do you supply the buyers?

No. Callworthy AEO builds and tunes the pages and the tracking. Arrangements with call buyers or advertisers are yours to make.
