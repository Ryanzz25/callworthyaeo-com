Pay per call pages built to make the phone ring

Pay per call models sell phone calls to the businesses that answer them. The model only works if the page gets found and the number is easy to tap. We build and optimize pages for exactly that.

Call first, everything else second

A call-focused page strips away anything that stands between a visitor and the phone. The number sits at the top, the message is short and the page loads fast on a mobile connection.

We write these pages carefully. Claims that cannot be backed up, such as licensing or response times, stay off the page.

How a call-driven page is built

  • A tap-to-call button in the header and again after the first screen.
  • Short sections for the service, the area and what the caller can expect.
  • Tracking numbers or event tracking, so calls are attributable to pages.
  • Clear disclosure when a site is a referral line rather than the contractor itself.
  • Local schema and area content so the page earns organic traffic over time.

Honest pages convert better over time

Pages that overpromise attract complaints and bounce back to search results. We write copy that describes the service accurately and sets the right expectation for the call.

That also protects the number itself. Directories and ad platforms are quick to remove pages that make claims they cannot support.
