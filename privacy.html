Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to answer your inquiry. This policy is effective 24 October 2026.

What the form collects

The contact form asks for a website address, a name, a work email, an optional goal and an optional message. We use those details to reply to you and to prepare the review you requested.

Who handles it

A third-party form service receives each submission and passes it to our email. Both act on our behalf to deliver your message. We do not sell your details or share them for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. Our network provider, Cloudflare, may set strictly necessary security cookies that protect the site from abuse.

How long we keep it

Inquiry emails stay for as long as they help us answer you or follow up on work you request. After that they are deleted.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent, email hello@callworthyaeo.com and we will act on the request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date.
