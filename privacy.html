Privacy policy

Callworthy AEO collects only what it needs to answer your inquiry. Effective 24 October 2026.

What the contact form collects

When you request a site review, the form collects your website address, name, work email, an optional main goal and an optional message. We use these details only to reply to you and to prepare the review you asked for.

Who processes it

Form submissions are handled by a third-party form processor and delivered to our email provider. Both act on our behalf to move your message to us. We do not sell your details or share them for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Our network provider, Cloudflare, may set strictly necessary security cookies to protect the site from abuse.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry emails for as long as they are needed to answer you and to follow up on work you request, then remove them.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the details you sent us, email hello@callworthyaeo.com and we will act on your request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
