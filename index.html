FAQ

Straight answers about AEO

What is AEO, and what does an AEO agency do?

AEO is answer engine optimization: shaping your pages, markup and listings so search answers and assistants can state facts about your business. An AEO agency writes answer-first service pages, adds FAQ and local business schema, corrects the Google Business Profile, aligns directory entries and keeps a log of what assistants say before and after the work.

Do I still need regular SEO?

Yes. Assistants draw on the same crawlable, well-written pages that rank in normal results. We plan SEO and AEO as one set of improvements rather than two separate retainers.

How is AI search different from a Google results page?

A results page lists links and lets the buyer choose. An AI answer picks a few businesses and explains them in a sentence or two. That makes clear, consistent facts about your company more valuable, because there is less room to be chosen.

What do you need from me to start?

A website address is enough. Access to Search Console and your business profile helps, and you can grant it later once you are comfortable.

Can you work alongside my developer or agency?

Yes. We can write tickets for your developer, supply copy to your agency or make the edits ourselves, whichever suits how you work.

How do you show progress?

We track rankings, map positions, a fixed set of buyer questions put to AI tools, and the calls and forms your site produces. Changes are written up in plain language.
