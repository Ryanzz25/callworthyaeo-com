FAQ

Questions, answered first

What is AEO?

AEO, or answer engine optimization, is the work of making your business easy for AI assistants and search answers to quote. It means clear service pages, consistent business details, schema markup and a Google Business Profile that agrees with your site, so ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can say what you do, where you do it and how to reach you.

What is the difference between AEO and SEO?

SEO gets your pages ranked in a list of links. AEO shapes the same pages so an AI answer can pull a clear statement from them. The groundwork overlaps heavily (crawlable pages, strong content, clean markup), so the two are done together rather than bought separately.

What is the difference between AEO and GEO?

AEO targets answers: featured snippets, voice results and AI summaries. GEO, or generative engine optimization, targets how large language models mention and recommend brands inside longer generated responses. In practice both come down to clear entities, quotable pages and trusted mentions, so we treat them as one workstream.

Do you implement the fixes, or only advise?

Both. You can take the ranked fix list to your own team, or have us do it. Page rewrites, schema markup, Google Business Profile updates, speed work and form or tool setup are all carried out by us.

How long until I see results?

Technical fixes and Google Business Profile changes can show up within weeks. Rankings and citations in AI answers usually take a few months to move, and nobody can promise a position or a citation. Each month we re-check rankings and AI answers and report what changed.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. White-label AEO covers audits, AI visibility benchmarking and implementation under your brand, with reporting you can pass to your own clients.
