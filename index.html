FAQ

Questions, answered first

What is AEO, and how is it different from SEO?

AEO (answer engine optimization) is making your business easy for AI assistants and search answers to quote. SEO gets your pages ranked in a list of links. AEO shapes those pages so ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can pull a clear answer from them: what you do, where you do it and how to reach you. The two share most of their groundwork, so they are done together.

Do you implement the fixes, or only advise?

Both. You can take the ranked fix list and give it to your own team, or have it done for you. Page rewrites, schema markup, Google Business Profile updates, speed work and form or tool setup are all things I carry out myself.

Which businesses are a good fit?

Local service businesses where a phone call or a form is the sale: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, pest control, dental, flooring, landscaping and similar trades. Agencies that need white-label AEO for their own clients are also a fit.

How long until I see results?

Technical fixes and Google Business Profile changes can show up within weeks. Rankings and citations in AI answers usually take a few months to move, and nobody can promise a position or a citation. Each month I re-check rankings and AI answers and report what changed.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. I offer white-label AEO for agencies: audits, AI visibility benchmarking and implementation under your brand, with reporting you can pass to your own clients.
