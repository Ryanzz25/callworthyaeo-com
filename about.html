About

Callworthy AEO helps service businesses||get found, quoted and called

We are a small search practice. We work on how local service companies appear on Google, on Maps and inside AI answers, and on the pages that turn a visit into a phone call. Our clients are in the US and Australia.

What we do

Our work sits in three places. The first is search and AI visibility: strategy, answer engine optimization, generative engine optimization and content planning. The second is local visibility, meaning the Google Business Profile, listings and area coverage. The third is the website itself, from fast static builds and WordPress tuning to forms and small tools that capture enquiries.

Everything begins with a review of what exists today. You can see the full range on the services page.

How we work

  1. 01

    Look

    We read your site, your profile and the answers assistants give about your trade.

  2. 02

    Agree

    You get a written list of what to change, in the order we would do it.

  3. 03

    Do

    We make the edits, or brief your team so they can.

  4. 04

    Report

    We re-check the same measures on a steady rhythm and choose the next job from what changed.

Who we work with

Businesses where a call or a form is the sale, including HVAC, plumbing, roofing, pest control, dental, landscaping, contractors, electricians. We also support agencies that need white label AEO behind their own brand.

Ground rules

  • Clear pages beat clever ones. If a person can find the service, the area and the number quickly, so can an assistant.
  • We make no promises about rankings or citations, because nobody controls them. We measure and report instead.
  • We never invent proof. Reviews, ratings, client names and results are not made up and are not added to a client site unless they are real.
  • The person who writes the review also makes the fixes.
