About

Callworthy AEO is the practice of Rehan Nawaz

Rehan is an SEO, AEO and GEO specialist working remotely from Bulacan, Philippines, with clients in the US and Australia. He has done hands-on SEO since 2021.

What the practice focuses on

Callworthy AEO gets service businesses found on Google, Google Maps and AI search (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Gemini), then makes sure that visibility turns into calls and form leads. It also builds the lead systems behind the site.

The work suits local service businesses such as HVAC, plumbing, roofing, pest control, dental, flooring and landscaping, and agencies that need white-label AEO.

How he works

  • Remote, with clients in the US and Australia.
  • Hands-on: the person who audits your site also makes the fixes.
  • Every site review is written by Rehan himself, with findings kept short and ranked by lead impact.
  • No promises about rankings or AI citations. Progress is measured monthly.
