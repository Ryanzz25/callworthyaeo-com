About

Callworthy AEO is an SEO and AEO practice for service businesses

Callworthy AEO is a search consultancy that gets local service businesses found on Google, Google Maps and AI search, then builds the pages and lead tools that turn that visibility into calls and form leads. It works with clients in the US and Australia.

What the company does

Most of the work falls into three areas. Search and AI visibility covers SEO strategy, answer engine optimization, generative engine optimization and content planning. Local visibility covers Google Business Profile and local SEO. Websites and lead systems cover fast static sites, WordPress tuning, forms wired to a CRM and small tools such as calculators.

Every engagement starts with a site review. The aim is a short list of fixes ranked by how much they affect calls, not a long export of every warning a crawler can produce. The full range is on the services page.

How it works

  1. 01

    Review

    A person reads your site, your Google Business Profile and the way search and AI answers describe you today.

  2. 02

    Agree the plan

    You receive a ranked list. You choose whether your own team carries it out or Callworthy AEO does.

  3. 03

    Implement

    Pages, markup, profile updates, speed fixes and forms are changed directly, in order of impact.

  4. 04

    Report monthly

    Rankings, AI answers, calls and leads are checked again each month, and the next fix is chosen from what moved.

Who it serves

Local service businesses where a phone call or a form is the sale: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, pest control, dental, landscaping and other contractors. Marketing agencies that need white-label AEO for their own clients are also a fit.

Principles

  • Plain pages beat clever ones. If a person can find the service, area and phone number in seconds, so can an AI assistant.
  • No promises about rankings or AI citations. Nobody controls those, so progress is measured and reported instead.
  • No invented proof. Reviews, ratings, client names and results are never made up, and are never placed on a client's site unless they are real.
  • The person who writes the audit is the person who makes the fixes.
