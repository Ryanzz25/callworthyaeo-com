Common problems that cost HVAC calls

  • One page for everything. Repair, installation, maintenance and duct work share a single page, so none ranks for its own search.
  • No emergency path. A visitor with no heat at night cannot find a phone number or a clear statement of what you cover.
  • Seasonal gaps. Heating pages go stale in summer and cooling pages in winter, just before demand returns.
  • Mismatched listings. Hours, service areas or phone numbers differ between the site and the Google profile.

What we fix first

  • Separate pages for repair, replacement, maintenance and each major equipment type you service.
  • A visible phone number and plain emergency information on every page, checked on mobile.
  • Google Business Profile categories, services and areas aligned with the site.
  • Answer-first FAQs on cost factors, repair or replace decisions and warning signs, ready for AI answers.
  • Seasonal refreshes scheduled before the heating and cooling peaks.

Where to start

Start with the page for the service that earns the most per call, usually repair or replacement, and the cities closest to your shop. Once those are ranking and tracked, add maintenance plans and the outer towns. The site review lists the order for your own market and flags any gaps between your site and your Google profile.
