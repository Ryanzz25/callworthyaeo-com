Common problems that cost plumbers calls

  • Everything on one page. Drain cleaning, water heaters and leak repair are lumped together, so a searcher for one sees no specific answer.
  • Slow mobile pages. People in a hurry leave if the number is not visible quickly.
  • A weak profile. The wrong primary category or missing services keep you out of the top Maps results.
  • Unclear coverage. Visitors cannot tell whether you serve their suburb or work after hours.

What we fix first

  • One focused page for each job type: leaks, drains, water heaters, fixtures and repiping.
  • Service area pages with real local detail, not copied text with the town name swapped.
  • A tap-to-call button and plain statement of coverage on every page.
  • Profile categories, services and photos matched to the website.
  • Schema markup and short answers to common plumbing questions for AI tools.

Where to start

The first month usually goes on the profile, the mobile call path and the three job pages that bring the most emergency work. Later work adds area pages and answers to common questions. If you run several lead sites rather than one company, the same structure can be repeated across them.
